# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Customer churn analysis for ABC Communications Ltd, completed as a business analytics project with AnalystLab Africa Consulting, to identify the major factors contributing to customer attrition and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

The analysis was performed using Excel


## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn directly affects revenue and customer lifetime value.

The business wants to understand:

- How many customers are leaving?
- Which customer segments have the highest churn?
- Which contract types experience the most churn?
- Does tenure influence customer churn?
- Which payment methods are associated with higher churn?
- Which services are linked to customer retention?


## 🎯 Objectives

•	Understand the characteristics of the customer base.

•	Identify customer segments with the highest churn.

•	Assess whether contract type influences retention.

•	Determine whether tenure is associated with loyalty.

•	Identify services associated with higher or lower churn.

•	Compare churn across payment methods.

•	Translate findings into practical customer-retention actions

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Contract Type

Month-to-month customers have significantly higher churn
compared with customers on one-year and two-year contracts.

### 2. Tenure

Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn,
indicating that the early customer lifecycle is a high-risk period.

### 3. Payment Method

Electronic check customers show a relatively high churn rate,
making payment behavior an important segment to investigate.

### 4. Internet Service

Fiber optic customers represent an important churn segment
and require further investigation into pricing and service experience.


## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Target Month-to-Month Customers

Introduce incentives for customers to move from month-to-month
contracts to one-year or two-year plans.

### 2. Strengthen Early Customer Retention

Create a 90-day onboarding and engagement program for new customers
to reduce early-stage churn.

### 3. Investigate Fiber Optic Churn

Review pricing, service quality and customer complaints among
fiber optic customers.

### 4. Encourage Automatic Payments

Provide incentives for customers to adopt automatic payment methods
where appropriate.

### 5. Build a Churn Risk Monitoring System

Use customer behavior and service information to identify customers
who are at high risk of leaving.
